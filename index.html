Ordering & Pick-up Requirements:
  • Ordering: Orders are taken throughout the week — so simply submit your order whenever suits you. Deadline for end of week orders is Thursday
  • Location: All orders must be collected in person from Clayton South. I will send address confirmation through text after your order has been placed.
  • Collection: Pick-up is Saturday mornings only, between 9am and 1pm. Please plan to collect your order within that window.
  • Payment: Cash only at pick-up (no cards or online payment).

Why Choose Fresh Pasta?

This pasta is as simple and as fresh as it gets. I don't use supermarket eggs or add any unnecessary ingredients. I make the orders on Friday so they'll have the longest possible shelf-life. You could probably just make it from home but I make them as cheap as possible so you don't have to.
